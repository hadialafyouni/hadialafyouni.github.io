My personal portfolio website, built from scratch with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
Why I built this
I got tired of trying to summarize everything I have done in a one page CV and a LinkedIn bio. I wanted something that actually shows who I am as a developer 
not just a list of skills and bullet points, but a real look at the projects I have built, the things I have learned, and where I am heading.

So I built it myself. No templates, no drag and drop builders. I figured if I was going to have a portfolio, it should itself be proof that I can build things.

What's inside
A full snapshot of who I am as a developer — projects, certifications, skills, and experience all in one place. Includes a working contact form and a CV download for anyone who wants to reach out or learn more.
